The city story in words
  1. Every city has a story. Every street, every neighbourhood, every voice matters.
  2. One small problem can affect an entire community. A teal car stops safely before a pothole on Al-Zaytoun Avenue, and the cars behind it queue up.
  3. See it. Report it. A neighbour photographs the pothole. A sample report attaches the photo, pins the location, names the category and sends it to the municipality.
  4. Get it fixed. A crew van leaves the depot, the road is patched and the report moves from Assigned to Resolved. Traffic flows again.
  5. Together, we make our city better. Explore RoadNa JO through the city landmarks, CityWorld, or the links above.

Transparent Governance

How RoadNa JO Works

A direct line from what residents see on the street to the crew that fixes it, with every step on the record.

  1. 01

    Step 01

    Report in Seconds

    Take a photo, pin the place on the map and pick a category. That is all it takes.

  2. 02

    Step 02

    Sent to the Right Team

    RoadNa suggests the category and priority, and your municipality sends a maintenance team.

  3. 03

    Step 03

    Follow It Until It Is Fixed

    See every update. A supervisor checks the before and after photos before a report is marked Fixed.
