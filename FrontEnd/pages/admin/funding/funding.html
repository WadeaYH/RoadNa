Requests and shortfalls

Funding requests

Sent by reviewers when a report has no budget. Critical repairs never come here: they are paid from the budget.

    Open campaigns

    A campaign closes by itself when it is fully funded. The last gift is capped at what is still needed.

      Short of target

      First choice: the municipality covers the rest. If it cannot, each donor chooses a refund or another repair. Donors who do not choose are refunded.

        Funded and repaired

        Funded reports go to the front of Review & assign. After the repair, donors see the cost, and any money left over goes back to their choice.
