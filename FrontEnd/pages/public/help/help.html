Emergency contacts

RoadNa JO is not an emergency service. If someone is hurt or in danger, for example from an open manhole, a fallen pole, a live wire or flooding, call 911, Jordan's unified emergency number, first. Then report the hazard here so crews can make it safe.

Support & feedback

Use the app to report a problem, follow it, or reopen it if a fix did not last. For help with your account, start from the sign-in page. The municipal support desk's phone number and email will be added before launch.

Privacy

We collect only what a report needs: the location, a description, photos, and the contact details used to send you updates. Your name is never shown on public reports or to field crews. Before a photo is published, faces and number plates are blurred. Personal data is handled under Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law, and you can ask to see or delete your data.

Terms of service

Report real problems in public spaces, with honest descriptions and your own photos. False, abusive or duplicate reports may be removed, and the reason is recorded. Response times are targets, not guarantees. Emergencies must go to 911.

Accessibility

RoadNa JO aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It supports keyboard use, screen readers, browser zoom and reduced motion. Every map has a list you can use instead. If something is hard to use, tell us through Support and we will fix it.

Service status

This is a frontend preview: sign-in, reporting and notifications are simulated, and nothing you enter is sent. The street map loads live from OpenStreetMap and needs an internet connection.

Open records

Anonymised reports, their status and response times are public. Every change to a report is logged, with who made it and when. Monthly performance summaries per district will be published here.
